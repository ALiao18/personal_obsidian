_[Willett et al. 2023](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41583-023-00731-8)_
1. Multiunit threshold crossings and spike band powers are recorded from the electrodes. 
2. neural activity is binned and smoothed on each electrode, RNN converts time series of neural activity into time series of probabilities of each phoneme and probability of interword token and blank token. Phoneme probabilities are combined with large vocab language model
3. Trials are defined as 
	1. _single word classification:_ 2-3s of preparation delay, 1s of "go" period, 1s of return/rest period, with 20 trials per condition for orofacial movements, and 16 trials per phoneme
	2. _sentence decoding trials:_ showing sentence, participant internal prep, 'go' cue, participant speaking the sentence, real time decoding, and participant pressing button. Avg speak rate 62 wpm, 80 trials/day for 125k word evaluation, 50 trials/day for 50 word eval.

	1. keys: 'sentenceText', 'tx1', 'tx2', 'tx3', 'tx4', 'spikePow', 'blockIdx'. tx are the number of times electrode voltage crossing a negative threshold. 
	2. The session contains 14 blocks, each with 20 sentences (trials), totaling 280 sentences. 
	3. For each session, first 2 blocks are held-out competition, last block is test, remainder is train. 
	4. Authors suggest using spikePow and tx1 (least selective threshold) together, first 128 columns only (area 6v), and to perform blockwise z-scoring of the data to prevent drift. 
	5. Each sentence has variable lengths (20ms bins). Data is recorded with 30kHz per channel, and is aggregates every 600 raw samples into a single number per channel. We can calculate time of trial via $478 \text{ bins} * 20ms=9.5s$
	6. the goal for streaming mode is C++ runtime receiving a bin every 20ms, with the rolling buffer accumulating rows until boundary detected. 
	7. all train data contains 8800 trials
