# General
I am a bridge between the students and the PIs. The students should be the main drivers of the project. 

It is my goal to 
1. make sure they know exactly what to do + exactly how to do it + provide unblockers. 
2. ensure they iterate relatively quickly
3. ensure they document and version control everything (credit assignment & troubleshooting)

Most of the communication regarding the project, progress, blockers, should go through the main channel. I will reply as much as I can. Of course, if there are personal concerns, etc, feel free to message me personally. 

Before each meeting, take attendance form, and after each meeting, fill in check-in form. All meetings should be recorded and invited with circleback@algoverseairesearch.org

# LLM sentence patterns to avoid
- Several paragraphs ending with "Together" or "Taken together"  
- Sentences with, "Rather than doing X, my paper does Y." (Why do we care about what your paper doesn't do?)  
- Sentences with, "I not only do A, but I also do B."  
- Words like "delve," "dive into," "tapestry," and more  
- Corporate-like phrases such as "In the grand scheme of things"
