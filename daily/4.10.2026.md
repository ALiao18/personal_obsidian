Also, a note-taking method I've been brainstorming for quite a while. Essentially, I find my ADHD brain constantly branching out thoughts. I can roughly divide all thoughts into 2 camps: constructive and introspective. 

For example, suppose I am writing the derivations of normal equations for linear regression. The actual derivations are the constructive thoughts. However, the proof of the assumptions are the "introspective" thoughts, for lack of the better word. Proofs include proof of convexity $\rightarrow$ unique solution, or why $i.i.d$ is required, etc. I think the notes can thus be constructed as a tree, where the constructive thoughts go up, and the introspective thoughts branch down.
